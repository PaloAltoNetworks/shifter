Expose read-only ACES package-backed catalog metadata (identity, contract/profile, package + lock digests, conformance status/report ref, bounded provenance summary, access and launchability state) through the CMS catalog API (`/api/v1/cms/catalog/`) and the scenario editor. Staff can inspect ACES entries and toggle their access overlay; ACES entries remain read-only (no authoring, YAML edit, clone, delete, or export). No raw ACES SDL, generated content, credentials, or provider payloads are exposed.
