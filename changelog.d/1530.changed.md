CI now enforces production-path quality ownership. `.github/quality-path-filters.yaml`
is a versioned, machine-readable contract that maps every production path to
blocking lint, security, and test jobs, and the `_quality.yml` path-detection
job rejects any unclassified changed path before it selects jobs. A new
`quality-path-ownership` ADR guard (ADR-004-R24) reconciles the contract against
the whole repository for estate completeness, ownership completeness, and
routing reachability. (#1530)
