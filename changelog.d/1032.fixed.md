Portal EC2 bootstrap now resolves the Auto Scaling group name at launch-hook completion time with bounded retry (IMDS `aws:autoscaling:groupName` instance tag first, `describe-auto-scaling-instances` fallback) instead of caching a single early lookup. Warm-pool and early instances no longer skip `complete-lifecycle-action` and get stuck in `Pending:Wait` until the ASG ABANDONs them (which broke instance-refresh convergence and the platform Deploy step). A configured launch hook that cannot be completed now fails bootstrap loudly instead of printing a false "bootstrap complete."
