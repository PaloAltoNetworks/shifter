Fixed the TechVault bake timing out at `create-image`. The bake used `aws ec2 wait image-available` (10-minute default), but the large TechVault AMI (100 GB root plus the full baked stack) takes 30-60 minutes to snapshot, so the wait failed with `Max attempts exceeded` and skipped recording the AMI in SSM even though it was finalizing normally. The bake now polls for availability with a 60-minute deadline and fails fast on a terminal image state.
