# UX-030: Responsive participant surfaces

## Statement

Every page a participant uses during an event, including challenge listing, challenge detail, scoreboard, range access, terminal, and submission flows, shall be usable down to a 360 px viewport width without horizontal scroll, content occlusion, or unreachable interactive elements. Organizer-facing surfaces may set a higher minimum viewport but shall document it.

## Rationale

Participants frequently use the platform from phones, during conferences, in the field, away from a workstation. A 360 px floor (Pixel-class small phones) is the realistic baseline for participant-facing surfaces. Organizer surfaces (scenario authoring, complex tables) realistically need more screen real estate, so a separate floor is acceptable as long as it's explicit.
