CTF magic-link registration now lands participants on their range page (`ctf:participant_range`) instead of the Mission Control dashboard, where a CTF participant would see no active range (MC ranges are separate from CTF ranges). The portal ASG also no longer fails `terraform apply` on large scale-ups: `wait_for_capacity_timeout` is set to `0` so the apply does not block on the ASG reaching capacity (new instances sit in Pending:Wait through their launch lifecycle hook, and warm-pool churn compounds it, exceeding Terraform's default 10-minute wait); readiness is gated by the instance refresh and the deploy's verify steps instead.
