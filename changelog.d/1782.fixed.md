Fixed intermittent and deterministic packer-bake failures caused by `producer | grep -q` pipelines under `set -o pipefail`: when `grep -q` closed the pipe on its first match, the producer took SIGPIPE writing the rest and `pipefail` reported the whole check as failed. This spuriously failed listener checks (`ss`), the systemd-resolved fallback-DNS assertion (`resolvectl status`, which deterministically broke every AWS kali/ubuntu base bake), the TechVault required-container check, and the scenario golden-verify health probe. Each site now captures the producer's output once and matches it with a here-string.
