Compressed the portal EC2 `user_data` with Terraform's `base64gzip()` so it stays under EC2's 25,600-byte encoded limit. The ~20 KB bootstrap script rendered to ~27 KB base64 and failed the platform apply with `InvalidParameterValue: Encoded User data is limited to 25600 bytes`; cloud-init transparently decompresses gzip user_data, dropping it to ~7.6 KB with no behavior change.
