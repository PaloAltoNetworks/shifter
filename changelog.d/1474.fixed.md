The engine deploy `validate` job now runs on the self-hosted runner class instead of `ubuntu-latest`. A GitHub-hosted runner-acquisition stall could previously cancel the job with zero steps started, which skipped the whole AWS Platform deploy stage through the fail-closed dependency chain. `validate` is fail-closed on `pull_request` (ADR-003-R5), keeps `contents: read` permissions only, and has a `timeout-minutes` backstop.
