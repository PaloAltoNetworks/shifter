ACES-native provisioning now genuinely delivers authored source-backed `file` and `directory` content into range guests: the payload is materialized from the digest-verified pack, promoted content-addressed to object storage, delivered over the authenticated guest channel, and verified in-guest by digest before the range becomes ready. `file` and `directory` are re-declared as backend content capabilities. Gated by `SHIFTER_ACES_NATIVE_PROVISIONING` (default off).
