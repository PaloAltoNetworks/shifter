Forward the post-deploy smoke's `SMOKE_*` environment into the portal container. `run-manage-on-portal` now accepts repeatable `--env KEY=VALUE` flags and renders them as `docker exec -e KEY=VALUE`, and `smoke-test.sh` forwards `SMOKE_TEST_USER_EMAIL` plus the per-variant `SMOKE_LINUX_AGENT_ID` / `SMOKE_WINDOWS_AGENT_ID` agent IDs. The container runs `run_post_deploy_smoke` under `docker exec`, which does not inherit the job environment, so those values previously arrived unset and the smoke failed on a required variable.
