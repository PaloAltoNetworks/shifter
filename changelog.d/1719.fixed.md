Fixed the GCP platform-core Terraform apply failing with `"account_id" ... must be between 6 and 30 characters long` for the `provisioner-launcher` workload service account. The account_id was `${replace(name_prefix, "-", "")}-${key}`, which for `name_prefix="shifter-gcp-dev"` produced `shiftergcpdev-provisioner-launcher` (34 chars, over GCP's 30-char limit; it also overflowed in prod at 32). Long workload keys now map to a bounded account_id suffix (`provisioner-launcher` -> `prov-launcher`); the logical key still names the GKE Kubernetes service account and the output entry, and every reference resolves through the SA's `.email`, so nothing downstream changes.
