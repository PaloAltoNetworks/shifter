Fixed the TechVault bake's container-count gate, which required 31 running `aptl-*` containers. The `techvault-operational` stack on aptl-labs 4.1.2 settles at 30 long-running containers plus a one-shot `aptl-cortex-index-init` that exits 0, so the bake-wait and golden-verify never reached 31 and timed out. Both checks now require 30.
