The TechVault scenario bake (`techvault-scenario-bake.yml`) now encrypts the bake host root volume at launch (`Encrypted:true`, matching the Polaris precedent) instead of relying only on the account's EBS-encryption-by-default posture, and adds a fail-closed pre-publish gate that verifies the produced AMI's EBS snapshots are encrypted before recording it in `/shifter/ami/techvault`. Previously an unencrypted golden AMI could be published, which the range provisioner's `ec2:Encrypted=true` IAM condition then correctly denied at launch, so TechVault ranges could not start.
