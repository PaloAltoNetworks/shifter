Grant the portal EC2 instance role `kms:GenerateDataKey`/`kms:Decrypt`/`kms:DescribeKey` on the user-storage S3 bucket CMK (scoped via `kms:ViaService = s3`). The bucket is SSE-KMS encrypted and its policy enforces the CMK, but the instance role was only granted the Secrets-Manager and SQS keys, so every challenge file-attachment upload and download failed with `AccessDenied` on `kms:GenerateDataKey`. Adds an `s3_kms_key_arn` variable and grant to `modules/portal/ec2`, wired from each environment's `aws_kms_key.portal_s3`.
