Added data-driven, workflow-aware launchability to the scenario registry: ACES package entries are launchable only when their source/contract/profile is supported, their refs/digests re-validate, they pass conformance, and they do not shadow an active legacy scenario. Range launch, CTF event selection, and CTF participant provisioning now consume launchable-only scenarios (with an explicit guard at range creation), while the staff catalog still shows non-launchable ACES entries for review. Legacy YAML and DB custom scenarios are unchanged.
